Executive summary

Core platform foundations are in place across backend (serverless API), data (Supabase), and portals (HR operational; employee scaffolded). Compliance is deterministic and auditable. The remaining work focuses on end‑to‑end background check orchestration (InformData), consent/PII controls, report viewing, and operational readiness. We estimate 4–6 weeks to MVP with current scope and guardrails.
